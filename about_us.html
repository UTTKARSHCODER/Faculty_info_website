ABOUT US :

Welcome to the Faculty Information Portal, a platform designed to simplify the way administrators access faculty-related information. Our goal is to make academic interactions smoother by providing easy access to faculty profiles, subjects taught, research interests, achievements, and contact details of the each and every faculty available in the institute.
This portal ensures transparency, accessibility, and efficiency in bridging communication between faculty members and Admin.

OUR MISSION :

To create a digital academic ecosystem where faculty and Admin stay connected, informed, and empowered.
The most important thing is that the Admin have the access to all the data of the faculty in just one click.This increase the ease for the Admin to have collectivelty have all the information at a time.
